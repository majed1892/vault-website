21–22 Oct 2026 · Jeddah

Vault is the Official Diamond Sponsor of the Saudi Maritime & Logistics Congress 2026

Vault Logistics joined the Saudi Maritime & Logistics Congress (VOYAGER 2026) at the Jeddah Superdome as Official Diamond Sponsor, connecting with industry leaders shaping the future of the maritime and logistics sectors.

Vault Logistics took part in the Saudi Maritime & Logistics Congress 2026 (VOYAGER 2026) as Official Diamond Sponsor, held on 21–22 October 2026 at the Jeddah Superdome.

The congress brings together more than 15,000 maritime professionals. Visitors met the Vault team at Stand B80 to explore new opportunities and the integrated solutions shaping the future of the maritime and logistics sectors.

The event organisers welcomed Vault as a logistics provider offering agile, reliable end-to-end supply chain solutions, built on long-term partnerships with its clients.

Vault and Badael sign a strategic agreement

Signed during the Supply Chain & Logistics Conference 2025 to develop the supply chain ecosystem and raise the efficiency of logistics operations in the Kingdom.

During the Supply Chain & Logistics Conference 2025, VAULT Logistics Solutions and Badael signed a strategic agreement aimed at developing the supply chain ecosystem and enhancing the efficiency of logistics operations in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Vault believes this collaboration will be a qualitative addition, contributing to operational excellence and supporting the Kingdom's aspirations in the logistics sector, and looks forward to many shared achievements ahead.

Dec 2025 · Riyadh

Vault signs MoU with Erth to lease 200,000 m² at the Swift Logistics Complex

A memorandum of understanding with Erth Company, signed during the Supply Chain & Logistics Conference 2025, to lease warehouses within the Swift Logistics Complex project.

During the Supply Chain & Logistics Conference 2025, Vault Logistics Solutions signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Erth Company as part of the Swift Logistics Complex project, to lease warehouses with a total area of 200,000 m².

The agreement strengthens Vault's storage capacity and supports the growth of its warehousing and logistics services for clients across the Kingdom.

Agreements
Dec 2025

Vault to operate a 30,000 m² facility in Riyadh with Makhzan Alarabia

An operating agreement with Almakhzan Alarabi Investment Company expands Vault's warehousing and logistics capabilities in the capital.

Vault signed an operating agreement with Almakhzan Alarabi Investment Company to manage and operate a 30,000 m² facility in Riyadh, enhancing its warehousing and logistics capabilities to support continued growth.

The agreement was signed by Mr. Faisal Al Jarbaa, Chief Executive Officer of Al Makhzan Al Arabi Investment Co., and Mr. Mohammed Malaikah, Chief Executive Officer of Vault Logistics.
